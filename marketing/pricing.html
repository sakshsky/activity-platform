Employee control belongs in every package.

Neutral screenshot deletion. Access to your own retained captures. Explicit acknowledgment for optional modules. No automated score penalties from activity, deletion or timing flags.

Screen captures can contain sensitive information. No keylogger or file-system scan is included, but visible text and documents may appear in screenshots or optional video. Capture policies and access decisions still matter.

Know the deployment boundaries.

This is a single-organization, self-hosted application. Optional video requires FFmpeg/FFprobe. Optional email uses your configured mail transport. There is no payroll, SSO, shared multi-tenancy, webcam capture or keystroke-content logging. Hosting, backups and any chosen FTPS/mail providers remain your responsibility.
